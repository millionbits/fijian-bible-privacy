Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Application: Fijian Bible (Ai Vola Tabu) • Package: com.millionbits.fijianbible

MillionBits ("we", "our", or "us") built the Fijian Bible (Ai Vola Tabu & Sere Ni Lotu) application as a free, offline-first religious and educational application. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to protecting your privacy and describes how our application handles your information in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

Summary: Fijian Bible (Ai Vola Tabu) is a completely offline, privacy-focused application. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any of your personal data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We respect your privacy. Fijian Bible does not collect, record, track, transmit, or share any personal information.

Specifically, the application does not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (such as your name, email address, phone number, or mailing address)
  • Precise or approximate physical location (GPS or network-based)
  • Device contacts, phonebook, media, photos, videos, or external files
  • Financial, billing, payment, or banking information
  • Persistent advertising identifiers (AAID) or tracking profiles
  • Audio or visual recordings

2. No Account or Registration Required

You can freely use the entire application without registering, creating an account, or providing any credentials. You are never asked to log in or link external social media accounts.

3. Local Device Data Storage

All data and preferences you create while using the application—such as your saved bookmarks, reading history, highlights, personalized notes, and reading preferences (font size, reading theme, and preferred parallel Bible translations)—are stored strictly locally on your physical device using local SQLite database storage and shared preferences.

This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any remote servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

4. User Data Control and Deletion

Because your user data is stored solely on your device, you have complete and exclusive control over it at all times. You can modify or delete your stored data whenever you wish by:

  • Removing individual bookmarks, notes, or highlights directly inside the app
  • Clearing the app storage and cache via your device settings (Settings > Apps > Ai Vola Tabu > Storage > Clear Data)
  • Uninstalling the Fijian Bible app from your device, which automatically erases all local data

5. Device Permissions

Fijian Bible operates without requesting access to sensitive device permissions:

  • No Sensitive Permissions: The app does not request access to Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, SMS, or External Shared Storage.
  • Zero Tracking: The application does not monitor your activity across other apps or websites.

6. Third-Party Services and Distribution

The app is distributed through the Google Play Store. When you download, update, or install the application, Google Play Services may collect standard diagnostic and technical telemetry necessary for app delivery and system integrity, as governed by Google's policies. For details, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

Our application does not include third-party advertising SDKs, commercial ad banners, analytical trackers, or behavioral profiling tools.

7. Children's Privacy

Fijian Bible (Ai Vola Tabu) is designed for a general audience and is family-friendly and safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or retain personal data from children or any other user group.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future application enhancements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be published directly on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top.
